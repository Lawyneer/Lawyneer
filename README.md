# Introverted . . .
## But willing to discuss taxes, patent law, and of course, coding!

By day, I'm a CPA and tax attorney who spent many years as a patent attorney before that.  Yes, I collect professional licenses and diplomas they way some people collect Pokémon cards, which I do not collect.  Along the way I picked up a BS and MS in [Mechanical Engineer](https://me.gatech.edu) from [Georgia Tech](https://gatech.edu), taught myself to code (had little help from CS 1310 back in the day), and now I build software to fix the things that annoy me.

## Why I Code

I build tools that solve my problems or just interest me.  Mostly this means accounting and tax software that does what I need without 400 features I'll never use.  If you've ever got frustrated at constant UI changes from software updates (NOBODY likes New Outlook or New Acrobat) or wish you could buy (not subscribe) to a basic software that just does what you want, we could probably hang out.  I LOVE the Unix philosophy that software should do one thing and do it well.

## Current Projects

- **[General Ledger App](https://github.com/Lawyneer/general-ledger-app)**:  A self-hosted, double-entry accounting app with journal entries, trial   balance, income statement, and balance sheet that lives in a single SQLite file.  Vanilla JS frontend, no build step, no nonsense.  Just a simple GL tool born out of my refusal to pay a monthly subscription for bloated accounting software.

- **[Tax Return Status Tracker](https://github.com/Lawyneer/return_tracker)**:  A lightweight web app for tracking tax returns from "not started" to completion, with full audit trail, overdue flags, and CSV export.  Because managing a busy season with sticky notes or in Excel is just wrong.

## Fun Projects
